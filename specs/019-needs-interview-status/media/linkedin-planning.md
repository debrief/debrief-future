# LinkedIn Summary: Deferred Idea Capture

Ideas arrive when you're busy with something else. The speckit workflow is getting a way to capture them quickly without a full interview — defer the details until you have time.

A new `needs-interview` status sits before `proposed` in the backlog. Quick capture with `/idea --defer`, then batch-process with `/interview` when you're ready. The interview itself is being redesigned around multiple-choice questions, one at a time — faster and less cognitive load than open-ended prompts.

Small workflow friction, compounding benefits over time.
