# LinkedIn Summary: Deferred Idea Capture

Ideas arrive at inconvenient times. You want to capture them before they evaporate, but detailed requirements gathering takes minutes you don't have.

Speckit's backlog workflow now supports quick capture with deferred interviews. Run `/idea --defer` to capture the essence in seconds. Later, run `/interview` to batch-process deferred items with structured multiple-choice questions. Preliminary scores assigned upfront get refined when you have time.

The workflow branches: quick capture when inspiration strikes, thorough detail when you have focus. Validation gates prevent incomplete ideas from reaching specification.
