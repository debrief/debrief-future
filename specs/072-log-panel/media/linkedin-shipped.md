Every plot tells a story of decisions. The Log Panel we shipped captures it: every measurement, calculation, and feature selection that led to your analytical conclusion, viewable in VS Code alongside the plot itself.

Three presentation modes let you show different perspectives of the same analysis. Filter by feature, search for specific decisions, review changes in sequence. Everything works offline and stores in your STAC catalogs.

Maritime analysis requires defensible reasoning—decisions must be auditable and explainable. When analysts can articulate their methodology with precision, their work becomes more trusted and more valuable. This panel is part of our broader provenance tracking approach in Future Debrief.
