VS Code's activity bar has six icons by default. For maritime analysts working with track data, five of them are noise.

This week I'm configuring the Debrief extension to hide those distractions automatically — leaving just Explorer and Debrief visible. The goal: when analysts open a plot, they see what matters for analysis, not what matters for software development.

It's a small change with an interesting constraint: VS Code has no API for this. We're modifying an internal setting directly. Feels a bit hacky, but the alternative is clutter. Trade-offs.
